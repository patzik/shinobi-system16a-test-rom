In these simulations only the 68000's reads were corrupted, so the screens themselves look clean. On a real board, bad video RAM usually also shows on screen as wrong colours, garbled text or broken graphics. A named chip is where the fault shows up: also check its socket, traces and bus buffers before replacing it.
